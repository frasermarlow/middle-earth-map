Every place plotted on the interactive map of Middle-earth, in the order the events happen. 128 places across the three Ages, drawn from The Silmarillion, The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings. Each name links to that spot on the map; the timeline shows the same events chronologically.

Two books have their own page: The Hobbit, whose fifteen places run the width of the map in a single year, and The Silmarillion, whose thirteen span all three Ages.

The Map x, y column gives pixel coordinates on the 7680 × 4386 base map, which is what the map's own deep links use: /?fly=x,y centres the map on a point. The same data, including the full descriptions, is published as locations.json.
