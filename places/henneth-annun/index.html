Henneth Annûn

Window of the Sunset

The Two Towers · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

A hidden refuge of the Rangers of Ithilien behind a curtain waterfall. Faramir brings Frodo and Sam here after capturing them in the wilds of Ithilien. Behind the falls, Faramir questions Frodo about the Ring and learns of Boromir's death — and unlike his brother, chooses not to seize the Ring.
