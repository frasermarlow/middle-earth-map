Field of Celebrant

Birth of Rohan

Appendices · Third Age 2510 · show on the map

In TA 2510, a great host of Easterlings invaded Gondor and pushed its armies back to the Field of Celebrant. In Gondor's darkest hour, Eorl the Young rode south from the far north with his entire people, the Éothéod, and routed the invaders. In gratitude, Steward Cirion granted Eorl the province of Calenardhon, which became Rohan.

Figures: Eorl the Young, Cirion
