Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli ride the Paths of the Dead beneath the White Mountains and emerge at the Stone of Erech. There Aragorn, as heir of Isildur, summons the oathbreaking Dead Men of Dunharrow to fulfil their ancient pledge. The ghostly army follows him south to Pelargir, where they overwhelm the Corsairs of Umbar in terror. Their oath fulfilled, the Dead are released and depart forever.
