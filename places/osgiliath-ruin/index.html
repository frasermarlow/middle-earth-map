Osgiliath

The Ruined Capital

The Two Towers · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

The ancient capital of Gondor, once spanning both banks of the Anduin, now a shattered ruin contested by Gondor and Mordor. Boromir led the defence that held the western bank, and it was a dream of Osgiliath's fall that prompted his journey to Rivendell. During the War of the Ring, Faramir's forces fight a desperate rearguard action as Sauron's armies pour across the river toward Minas Tirith.
