The Shire

Homeland of the Hobbits

Appendices · Third Age 1601 · show on the map

A fertile, well-ordered region in Eriador settled by Hobbits in TA 1601 when the brothers Marcho and Blanco received a land-grant from King Argeleb II of Arthedain. Bounded by the Brandywine River to the east, the Far Downs to the west, and roughly the East Road to the south, the Shire was divided into four Farthings — North, South, East, and West. Its chief town was Michel Delving on the White Downs, seat of the Mayor. The Shire's pastoral peace was quietly maintained by the Rangers of the North — Dúnedain who guarded its borders without the Hobbits' knowledge. After the War of the Ring, King Elessar made the Shire a Free Land under the protection of the Northern Sceptre and forbade Men from entering it.
