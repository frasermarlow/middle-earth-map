Dimrill Dale

The Mirrormere

The Fellowship of the Ring · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

Emerging grief-stricken from Moria after Gandalf's fall, the Fellowship pauses at the Dimrill Dale. Gimli looks into the Mirrormere (Kheled-zâram), the sacred lake where Durin the Deathless first saw a crown of stars reflected about his head, and the sight strengthens his resolve.

Figures: Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Boromir
