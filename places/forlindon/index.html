Forlindon

Northern Lindon

The Silmarillion · Second Age 1 · show on the map

Forlindon, or North Lindon, is the land north of the Gulf of Lune and west of the Blue Mountains. A surviving remnant of ancient Beleriand after the War of Wrath drowned most of that continent, it was part of Gil-galad’s realm in the Second Age. By the Third Age the region was sparsely populated, its Elven inhabitants slowly departing for the Undying Lands.
