Sea of Núrnen

The Slave Lands

Appendices · Second Age 1000 · show on the map

An inland sea in southern Mordor, fed by rivers flowing down from the Ephel Dúath and Ash Mountains. The fertile lands of Nurn surrounding it were tended by vast armies of slaves who grew food to feed Sauron's war machine. After the fall of Barad-dûr, King Elessar freed the slaves and granted them the lands of Nurn as their own.
