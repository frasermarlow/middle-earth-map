Carn Dûm

Fortress of Angmar

Appendices · Third Age 1300 · show on the map

The iron fortress of Carn Dûm was the capital of Angmar, the realm the Witch-king founded around TA 1300 with the sole purpose of destroying the Dúnedain of the North. For nearly 700 years, Angmar waged war against the successor kingdoms of Arnor, eventually destroying all three.
