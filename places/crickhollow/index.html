Crickhollow

The Conspiracy Unmasked

The Fellowship of the Ring · Third Age 3018 · show on the map

Frodo's house in Buckland, purchased as a cover story for leaving the Shire. Here Merry, Pippin, Sam, and Fatty Bolger reveal they have known about the Ring all along and insist on joining Frodo's journey. After the company departs into the Old Forest, a Nazgûl attacks the house at night. Fatty Bolger escapes and sounds the Horn-call of Buckland, the first time it had been blown in generations.

Figures: Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Fatty Bolger, the Nazgûl
