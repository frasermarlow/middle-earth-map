Pelargir

Capture of the Corsair Fleet

The Return of the King · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

Aragorn leads the Dead south from Erech to the ancient port of Pelargir, where the Corsairs of Umbar have anchored their fleet. The Dead sweep through the Corsairs in terror, and Aragorn seizes the ships. He releases the Dead Men from their oath, and they vanish at last. Aragorn then mans the ships with Rangers, men of Lebennin and Lamedon, and sails up the Anduin to the Pelennor Fields.

Figures: Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli, the Dead Men of Dunharrow, the Corsairs of Umbar
