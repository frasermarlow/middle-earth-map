Annúminas

Capital of Arnor

Appendices · Second Age 3320 · show on the map

Built by Elendil on the shores of Lake Evendim after the Downfall of Númenor, Annúminas served as the first capital of the North-kingdom of Arnor. As the Dúnedain dwindled, the city was abandoned and fell into ruin. After the War of the Ring, Aragorn restored it as his northern seat of power.
