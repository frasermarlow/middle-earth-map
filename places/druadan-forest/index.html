Drúadan Forest

The Wild Men

The Return of the King · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

When the road to Minas Tirith is blocked by Sauron's forces, Ghân-buri-Ghân of the Drúedain (Wild Men) guides the Rohirrim through secret paths in the ancient forest, allowing them to reach the Pelennor Fields in time. In gratitude, Aragorn later grants the Drúadan Forest to the Wild Men forever.

Figures: Ghân-buri-Ghân, Théoden, Éomer, Merry
