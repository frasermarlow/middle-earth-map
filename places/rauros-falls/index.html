Rauros Falls

Boromir's Funeral

The Fellowship of the Ring · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

The great waterfall of Rauros on the Anduin, near the hills of Emyn Muil. After Boromir falls defending Merry and Pippin, Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli place his body in an Elven boat and send it over the falls as a funeral rite — a solemn farewell to the son of Gondor.
