Fanuidhol

Cloudyhead

Appendices · First Age 1 · show on the map

Fanuidhol, Cloudyhead (Bundushathûr in Khuzdul), is the easternmost of the three peaks above Moria, overlooking the Dimrill Dale and the Mirrormere from the south. Together with Caradhras to the north and Celebdil to the west, these three mountains form the crown above the ancient Dwarven kingdom of Khazad-dûm.
