Dol Amroth

City of the Swan Knights

Appendices · Third Age 3019 · show on the map

The coastal stronghold of Dol Amroth was the seat of the Princes of Belfalas, who claimed descent from the Elves. Prince Imrahil led the renowned Swan Knights to the defence of Minas Tirith, and his company was among the most valiant at the Battle of the Pelennor Fields. He ruled Gondor briefly after Denethor's death.

Figures: Imrahil, Galador
